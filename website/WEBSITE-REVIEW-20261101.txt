The retained architecture is a Node-generated static website. The entry point
is website/scripts/build.mjs, with shared business identity in site.config.mjs,
six service definitions in services.mjs, the detailed street-lighting content
and template, and new editorial page data in page-content.mjs. Generated HTML
and the existing assets remain under website/dist. No framework migration or
new browser dependency was introduced.

Visitor experience

The existing VOLTRONIX red, dark neutral, cream, Manrope typography, logo and
photographs are retained. Navigation now provides Services, About, Resources,
Contact and a direct quotation path. Native disclosure menus work with a
keyboard and on small screens. The footer keeps the published company contact
details together and explains the independent relationship to RTA.

The homepage introduces lighting and electrical works clearly, then offers
three useful starting points: street lighting, underground cabling and a
quotation brief. Eight broader work-topic cards were removed because they
were not established completed projects or supported service evidence. The
scroll-reveal dependency was removed from the page. Content is available in
normal document flow. Existing background video now loads only after the
visitor chooses to play it.

Authority-logo animation was replaced by useful qualification, company-profile
and planning-guide links. Representative images remain labelled as such;
they are not presented as completed RTA projects or client endorsements.

Business and content boundaries

VOLTRONIX Contracting LLC is the contractor. rtacontractor.ae is its service
channel, not a separate legal business or an RTA government service. The site
does not promise authority decisions, approval times, pricing or project awards.

The supplied certificate remains described as conditional prequalification for
road street lighting, excluding tunnel lighting, with an AED 5,000,000 value
limit. Registration, expiry, conditions and the absence of a fresh portal-status
check remain visible on the detailed service page. This has not been expanded
into blanket RTA approval. Other specialist lighting topics invite scope review;
they do not inherit the street-lighting qualification.

No new ISO, licence, award, client, testimonial, completed-project, performance
statistic or legal-compliance claim was added. Current ISO renewal evidence is
still required: the published corporate material displays validity to September
2026 and should not be used as proof of current certification. New project case
studies and testimonials were not fabricated to fill a page.

The guides explain what buyers can prepare and ask about. They distinguish
company qualification from project-specific approvals and link to official
resources instead of stating changing procedural fees or times. These public
resource URLs returned HTTP 200 during this review:

Search and machine-readable content

Every indexable page has its own title, description, HTTPS apex canonical,
visible heading and crawlable internal links. Authored public page links and
sitemap entries use trailing slashes. The sitemap contains all 19 indexable
routes and excludes the 404. The existing 53-byte Google verification file is
preserved exactly. Robots rules continue to allow public pages.

Organization identity, breadcrumbs and supported Service schema remain scoped
to the documented business and service facts. New AboutPage, ContactPage,
CollectionPage, visible FAQ Question/Answer and guide Article data describe
the actual pages. No invented review ratings or article publication dates were
added. Business sameAs is limited to the corporate website; existing public
social links are not treated as independently verified business identities.
Static HTML exposes the substantive content without requiring JavaScript.
These are technical readiness improvements, not indexing or AI-citation claims.

Enquiries and measurement integration

The original synchronous Zoho embed URL/form identity, fallback phone/email
links and chat/assistant coordination are retained. Contact and quotation
pages reuse that provider embed once per page. No new form automatically
intercepts or duplicates the provider's submission.

The f04 assets/tracking.js, tracking.config.mjs and contact-widgets.js are
byte-for-byte unchanged. The existing exact, form-scoped Zoho acknowledgment
contract and PII-free bridge events remain the tracking baseline. An intention,
assistant opening or visible splash is not promoted into a confirmed form lead.

The separate marketing agent supplied the public disabled controller/config,
attribution and lead-client modules. The build places the marketing JSON/module
before the retained tracking adapter, versions both imported dependencies, then
hashes the final entry module. Static checks verify those exact content hashes
and require its public landing-path privacy allowlist to equal the authored
routes plus the literal /404.html path.

All new activation flags remain off. The current build preserves the existing
GTM loader pair and does not display an active consent banner or preference
button. marketing-build-policy.mjs rejects partially requested activation.
Only a fully matching reviewed configuration, positive coordinated container
version, granular tag gates and URL/referrer-policy gate can omit that pair
and delegate sole GTM loading to the controller. Those flags are review gates;
their presence is not independent proof of provider behavior or legal approval.

The prepared controller concerns GTM-managed measurement. It does not block
the independent Zoho form, SalesIQ or assistant services. The cookie information
page does not promise that all third-party connections are prevented. The
separate lead service remains disabled pending approved credentials, mappings,
provider/HUB contracts and deployment review.
