## Google, Bing and downstream discovery

Google's ordinary crawl, index and snippet eligibility applies to its AI features. No special AI schema or `llms.txt` is required by the cited guidance. Useful original technical answers and consistent documented company/service facts matter; invented approvals, project history and ratings are excluded. [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), [Google AI optimization guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

The current Google optimization guide links a Generative AI performance report while AI-features guidance also describes aggregate Web reporting. Detailed methodology did not render in the public check. Verify actual property/report availability; do not invent a missing or available report. Bing documents AI Performance and Topics Portfolio beta; actual account availability and metric meanings still need inspection. Citation counts are not visits or leads. [Google report entry](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139), [Bing AI Performance](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/), [Bing Topics beta](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/10/introducing-topics-portfolio-beta-in-ai-performance/).

Prioritize Google and Bing. Yahoo directs site submissions through Bing; DuckDuckGo uses Bing for many traditional links alongside other sources; Ecosia varies among providers. Correct Bing setup can help downstream discovery but does not guarantee it. Brave documents its crawler/crawlability behavior separately. Yandex, Baidu and Naver remain conditional on actual audience/language demand. [Yahoo](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN2217.html), [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/duckduckgo-help-pages/results/sources), [Ecosia](https://support.ecosia.org/article/579-search-results-providers), [Brave](https://search.brave.com/help/brave-search-crawler).

## Crawler policy

The public robots file permits public crawling. An anonymous GET returning 200 does not prove Googlebot, Bingbot or an AI crawler can pass the CDN/WAF, and does not establish an indexed page. Verify legitimate bot identity/IPs and logs without weakening security broadly.

OpenAI's `OAI-SearchBot` search discovery and `GPTBot` training controls are independent. `ChatGPT-User` represents user-initiated activity rather than automatic search crawling. Perplexity documents its search crawler separately from training. Current Claude crawler documentation did not retrieve during this audit, so no speculative directives are introduced. Owner policy determines training versus search access; no AI account or guaranteed citation results from allowing a crawler. [OpenAI bots](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), [Perplexity crawlers](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers).

Bing announced separate training/grounding preferences on 30 October 2026 with changes beginning **1 February 2027**. These are future policy decisions at this review date, not a completed currently effective integration. Do not block grounding merely to opt out of training. [Bing announcement](https://blogs.bing.com/search/2026/10/your-content-your-choice-clearer-preferences-for-training-and-grounding/).

## IndexNow and operational readiness

[operations/search/indexnow.mjs](../search/indexnow.mjs) defaults to a dry run. It prepares canonical routes, requires an owner-managed key and live same-host ownership proof, caps the response body and uses an explicit submission mode. No live submission is claimed. The prepared implementation chooses a 32-character hexadecimal key, within the protocol's permitted range. Deploy and verify a real matching UTF-8 proof file over HTTPS rather than accepting a static-host HTML fallback. HTTP receipt is not indexing or citation. [IndexNow protocol](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation), [IndexNow FAQ](https://www.indexnow.org/faq).

CI, dependency review and a public monitor workflow are prepared in source. No production schedule, reporting connection or alert recipient is installed by these files. Monitoring and reporting must use actual results, reviewed recipients and appropriate access. Public performance data is unavailable.
