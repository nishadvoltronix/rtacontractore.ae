## Channel sequence

1. **Google Search:** review the existing campaign and accepted-form goal first. Use the focused street-lighting page, Dubai presence targeting proposal, verified English-language coverage and relevant exact/phrase intent. Assess partner traffic and expansion using actual results later.
2. **Microsoft Search:** reuse validated intent only after advertiser identity/account access, UET goal, consent and billing/publication decisions. The unfinished native conversion-goal flow is not a saved goal.
3. **LinkedIn:** establish company-page and Campaign Manager ownership. Prepare technical procurement/project-scoping content before paid B2B tests; targeting reach and cost need account evidence.
4. **Meta retargeting:** requires business ownership, valid destination/pixel, actual consent behavior and sufficient eligible audience. No Meta Pixel, CAPI or audience is claimed implemented.
5. **YouTube:** prepare genuine technical explanations and authorised project material. Existing graphics do not establish an official channel or campaign.

The [platform matrix](platform-matrix.md) classifies TikTok, Snapchat, X, Pinterest, Reddit, Quora, Taboola, Outbrain, Amazon and Yandex Direct as conditional or lower priority for this Dubai B2B scope. This is a relevance decision, not proof that those channels cannot work. No unrelated account was created to fill the list.

## Claims and measurement gates

Search wording such as “RTA approved contractor” can express buyer intent; it does not permit an unqualified approval claim in an advertisement. Copy retains the independent Voltronix identity and the documented conditional scope. General civil works, standalone NOC assistance, maintenance expansion and new industry/project claims stay deferred until evidence and landing-page fit are verified.

Before applying a draft: confirm live HTTPS/canonical redirects; actual services and current credentials; owner-approved targeting, keywords and spend; one correct conversion owner; accepted-form measurement and platform processing; contact routing; and consent/audience eligibility. The fresh Google Ads form action is still reported **Misconfigured**, secondary, count One and no value. The draft pack cannot be described as having repaired that reporting state.

Use actual source reports to decide which queries produce accepted enquiries, qualification and projects. Telephone/WhatsApp/email clicks, chatbot openings, quote-button clicks and profile-PDF openings remain intents. Do not optimize a bidding goal around these as though they were confirmed qualified leads. See [tracking proof](tracking-verification.md) and the [lead architecture](lead-architecture.md).
