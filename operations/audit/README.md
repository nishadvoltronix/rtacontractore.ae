The public matrix covers every named search engine, AI service, advertising platform, analytics product, social network, directory and procurement portal in the master request. A row is a platform **role**: Google Ads, for example, appears both as an advertising service and as a conversion destination. Its 100 rows are not 100 independently authenticated account audits. Suitability reflects the requested Dubai B2B priorities; it is not measured return on investment. An unverified profile is never labelled absent merely because a public search did not establish it.

Evidence is classified throughout:

- **Executed:** a local build/test, public read-only observation or completed native account action reported and read back by the operator.
- **Prepared:** source code, draft asset, disabled integration or proposed configuration; it has not thereby taken effect externally.
- **Gated:** access, current business proof, secure credentials, provider contract, coordinated tracking or explicit publication/deployment approval is still required.

The public website and corporate contact agree on the legal name, published Dubai Investment Park address and business contacts. The contractor is independent of Dubai RTA. Its supplied conditional road-lighting prequalification is kept within its documented scope and exclusions; current authority portal standing has not been independently established. Corporate material displays ISO validity ending in September 2026: request current evidence before asserting current certification. The matching Yellow Pages listing also contains legacy NSTS text and needs verified owner review. See the cited public evidence in the matrix.

No numeric visitor, enquiry, advertising, revenue or attribution result is claimed in this public repository. The private dashboard has five authored views and a bounded, authenticated native Search Console export; the raw export and reported observations remain outside this checkout. Unconnected business metrics stay unavailable. A browser SDK call, configured tag, CRM field or account link is not proof of processed attribution, a qualified lead or revenue. [Tracking verification](tracking-verification.md) explains these separate stages.

These documents intentionally omit private server/network addresses, SSH locations, private account locators, credentials, private bindings, customer/test identities and raw lead data. Supporting private operational evidence remains outside the public repository. This report is a dated review snapshot; it does not replace a fresh preflight or grant production approval.

[Machine-readable review status](review-status.json) records executed/prepared/gated states without private bindings or fabricated performance. The final local integrated run passed 137 tests after independent hardening. Linux permissions and the new remote deployment plan remain separate preflight requirements.
